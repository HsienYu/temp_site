# portrait 2011

year: 2011
medium: recycled E-waste, wood, bottle, plastic tube, motor, microcontroller, sensor, pump, custom electronic circuit
dimensions: size 20cm(L) * 8cm(W) * 26cm(H)

\[ portrait 2011 ] 肖像2011

Brief:

\[ portrait 2011 ] is a robotic head made from recycled E-waste, and it senses how many people are in the exhibition space. If there are people in the exhibition space, it will start to move its face. But if no one is in the exhibition space, it will start to cry.

“Only crying behind you”
