# Artist’s daily job

year: 2015
medium: custom website, computer, “apply and submit”

\[ Artist’s daily job ]

Brief:

\[ Artist’s daily job ] is an artwork in the form of an application form that has no ending and no submit button. Even when people try to finish the form, new fields on different topics keep appearing, so you can never finish it.

It is a kind of metaphor for how artists might spend more time filling in applications than making artworks.
