# isISis

year: 2015
medium: custom software, internet image data, generative video installation
dimensions: size variable

\[ isISis ]

Brief:

\[ isISis ] is a generative video artwork that collects image data related to ISIS and the Japanese manga iS’ from the internet, and moshes them together by data bending to generate random glitch video.
