# Dish on Fish

year: 2014
medium: plate, servo motor, wire, LED
dimensions: size 42cm (R) * 3cm (H)

\[ Dish on Fish ] 盤中魚

Brief:

Copy Series - (1)

This series of works presents some classic works by other artists in different media. "dish on fish" is a work copying "fish on dish" by Goang-Ming Yuan.

\[ Dish on Fish ] is a robotic installation: a plate that moves constantly on the floor.
