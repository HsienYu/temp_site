# Hobo

year: 2015
medium: recycled e-waste, Raspberry Pi, camera module, plastic, wood, microcontroller, speaker, sensors
dimensions: size 40cm (L) * 26cm (W) * 160cm (H)

\[ Hobo ]

“Money to avoid being bothered”

Brief:

People sometimes give money to hobos just to avoid being bothered by them, without really caring about their lives and situations. \[ Hobo ] is a robotic installation that tries to represent this theme in the metropolis. It chases visitors and makes loud noises to bother them unless they throw coins into the installation.
