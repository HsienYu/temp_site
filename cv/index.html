Hsien-Yu CHENG (b. 1984, Kaohsiung, Taiwan; lives and works in Taipei)

HsienYu Cheng (b. 1984, Kaohsiung, Taiwan) is an artist and software developer whose practice examines how technological systems act upon people and their environments. Working with software, electronic installations, machine learning, and experimental devices, he constructs systems that observe, measure, interpret, restrict, or intervene in human behavior, emotion, and the environment, often revealing their logic through humor and absurdity. His work is frequently exhibited in Europe and Asia, and has been presented at institutions and platforms including Taipei Fine Arts Museum, YCAM, Ars Electronica, Sónar, and Ludwig Museum Budapest. He received the First Prize of the Taipei Digital Art Award in 2014, the First Prize for New Media Art at the Kaohsiung Art Award in 2017, the Tung Chung Prize in 2019, the Visual Arts Award of the 19th Taishin Arts Award in 2021, and an S+T+ARTS Nomination / Honorary Mention at Ars Electronica in 2023.

鄭先喻

鄭先喻（1984 年生於台灣高雄，現居住、工作於台北）是藝術家與軟體開發者，他的創作探討科技系統如何作用於人及其所處的環境。他運用軟體、電子裝置、機器學習與實驗性設備，建構能觀察、測量、詮釋、限制或介入人類行為、情感與環境的系統，並常以幽默與荒謬揭露這些系統的運作邏輯。作品經常於歐洲與亞洲展出，曾在臺北市立美術館、山口情報藝術中心（YCAM）、奧地利電子藝術節、巴塞隆納 Sónar 及布達佩斯路德維希美術館等機構與平台發表。曾獲 2014 年台北數位藝術獎首獎、2017 年高雄獎新媒體藝術首獎、2019 年銅鐘藝術賞、2021 年第十九屆台新藝術獎視覺藝術獎，並於 2023 年獲奧地利電子藝術節 S+T+ARTS Nomination / Honorary Mention。
