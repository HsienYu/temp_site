# Untitled: The result

year: 2016
medium: recycled E-waste, FRP structure, LCD module, energy harvesting module, xenon light, moss, agar, carbon fabric, custom electronic circuit
dimensions: size variable

\[ Untitled: The result ]

“ Light \<-> Light ”

Brief:

\[ Untitled: The result ] is an installation that transfers bio-energy into electric current. The installation will trigger a xenon flash light at intervals that depend on how active the oxidation is in those chambers. Each trigger will exhaust all of the power that has been harvested. The artist is trying to present the process of energy transformation, expressing natural light -> artificial light, and the difficulty of energy management.
